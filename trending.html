Trending shawls this winter

Updated October 2026. This list comes from fashion news, runway reports and Pakistani retailer collections. It shows what is getting attention, not exact sales numbers.

Women

Light layers over heavy coats

Women are choosing shawls, ponchos, capes and shrugs they can add or remove through the day, instead of bulky clothing.

The shroud and stole shape

Fall/Winter 2026 runway shows in New York featured wrap-like stoles and draped shroud shapes in cashmere, knit and silk. Shawl-like pieces are being treated as a polished, grown-up layer.

Neutrals first, bold colour second

Beige, grey and black stay popular. Bold colours are used as a playful accent.

Embroidered and velvet shawls for events

Pakistani brands feature velvet embroidered, pashmina, jacquard and Kashmiri shawls for wedding season. Textured 3D embroidery and eco-friendly embroidery are named as styles to watch in 2026. Search interest in embroidered shawls usually peaks at the end of the year and in early January.

Patterned and textured wool for daily wear

When the shawl is the main accessory, women pick a patterned or textured wool piece over a plain outfit.

Men

Handloom shawls

Handloom shawls drape naturally and hold their shape. Winter menswear is moving towards craft and quieter style.

Shawl plus waistcoat

Layering is the main idea for winter weddings. A waistcoat under a shawl on a shalwar kameez is a common combination.

Texture over shine

For winter weddings, texture has replaced shine as the look to aim for. Plain charcoal velvet with fine gold embroidery, and wool with woven borders and fringes, are popular picks.

Muted and deep colours

Royal red, bottle green and black are typical winter colours in Pakistan. Olive, grey and charcoal suit office and formal use.
